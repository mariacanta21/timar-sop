Goal Statement

The purpose of this SOP is to guide the designated team member (David or Ben) through the process of conducting an effective Onboarding Session with a new client. It outlines the key objectives for the session, essential topics to cover (reviewing briefs, setting communication expectations, and outlining the search strategy), and how to transition into the active property search phase. This ensures every onboarding session is comprehensive, builds client confidence, and seamlessly moves them into the next stage of working with us.

Importance

The Onboarding Session marks the official commencement of our client relationship and the active property search. A well-conducted session is fundamental for setting clear expectations, confirming all client requirements, establishing communication protocols, and formally kicking off the buyer's journey. It ensures that both the client and our team are fully aligned, equipped with all necessary information, and ready to proceed efficiently - which is vital for a smooth process and successful outcome.

Onboarding Session Process

The Onboarding Session is the formal kick-off to the client's property search with us. It typically occurs after the client agreement is signed and the initial invoice is paid.

1

Session Preparation (Before the Call)

  • Review Client Documents: Thoroughly review the client's signed Client Agreement, their completed Fact Finder, and all notes from the Pre-Qualification and Strategy Sessions.
  • Access Client Folder and Trello: Have the client's Google Drive folder and Trello checklist open and ready for reference and updates during the call.
  • Prepare Buyer's Brief: Ensure the initial Buyer's Brief (summary of client requirements) is drafted and ready for review with the client.
  • Confirm Logistics: Ensure the Zoom link is working if virtual, or the meeting room is prepared if in-office.
Do not start the session without reviewing the Fact Finder first. Clients expect you to remember their requirements - arriving unprepared damages trust immediately.
2

Open the Session

Warm Welcome

"Hi [Client Name/s], welcome! It's fantastic to officially have you on board. Congratulations on taking this exciting step!"
"Thanks for completing the agreement and getting the initial invoice processed. We're truly excited to work with you."

Confirm the Agenda

"As per the email, today we'll be diving into your detailed brief, setting up our communication rhythm, and outlining exactly how we'll find your ideal property. Does that sound good?"
3

Review the Buyer's Brief

Present the drafted Buyer's Brief to the client and work through it together.

"We've put together an initial brief based on our previous discussions and your Fact Finder. Let's go through it together to ensure we've captured every detail accurately and that it perfectly reflects what you're looking for."

Discuss and confirm each of the following:

  • Property Type and Features: Bedrooms, bathrooms, land size, garage, condition (move-in ready vs. renovation potential), key features such as natural light, kitchen style, outdoor space.
  • Location and Suburbs: Target suburbs, desired proximity to amenities (schools, transport, shops), and any areas to avoid.
  • Budget and Financing: Reconfirm the exact budget and finance pre-approval status.
  • Timeline: Confirm the preferred purchase timeline and any key drivers (e.g., school start date, end of lease).
  • Purpose: Owner-occupier, investment, or commercial.
  • Non-Negotiables vs. Nice-to-Haves: Clarify absolute must-haves versus features the client is flexible on.

Make any necessary adjustments to the brief during the session and confirm the final version before moving on.

4

Set Communication Expectations

"To ensure we keep you fully informed, let's discuss how and when we'll communicate."
  • Primary Communication Channel: Confirm preferred method - phone, email, or WhatsApp.
  • Frequency of Updates: Agree on update cadence (e.g., weekly summary, or as-soon-as-a-property-arises).
  • Reporting: Explain how property presentations will be delivered (e.g., Google Sheet, detailed reports).
  • Response Times: Outline expected response times for client queries.
  • Availability: Confirm best times for calls and discussions.
Set clear expectations here to prevent over-communication or under-communication complaints later. Clients who feel out of the loop are the hardest to manage.
5

Outline the Search Strategy

"Now that we're aligned on the brief, let's walk through how we'll actively find your property."
  • Off-Market and Pre-Market Access: Reiterate strong agent relationships and how properties are accessed before they hit the general market. Emphasise this as a key advantage in the Tasmanian/Hobart market.
  • Active Sourcing: Explain the process of calling agents, researching upcoming listings, and tapping into the network.
  • Property Presentation: Describe how suitable properties will be presented (e.g., detailed reports including photos, floor plans, and initial assessment).
  • Inspections: Explain how inspections will be managed - initial inspections are conducted first and the client is only brought to properties that meet their specific brief.
  • Due Diligence and Negotiation: Briefly reiterate the role in due diligence (building, pest, legal) and expert negotiation on the client's behalf.
6

Answer Client Questions

Dedicate ample time for the client to ask any questions about the process, the market, or the service.

"Do you have any questions at this stage about our process, the search, or anything else?"

Do not rush this section. Questions here prevent misunderstandings and complaints later.

7

Close and Confirm Next Steps

Confirm Readiness

"Does everything we've discussed today feel clear, and are you comfortable with our approach?"
"Are you ready for us to officially commence the active search for your property?"

Set Next Actions

  • Our actions: Finalise the Buyer's Brief, circulate internally, and start actively reaching out to the agent network. Confirm when the first update will be sent (give a specific day or date).
  • Client actions: Remain responsive to communications and notify us if any preferences change.

Positive Close

"We're excited to partner with you on this journey. We'll be in touch very soon!"
8

Document in WayHub and Trello

WayHub

  • Immediately after the session, add a comprehensive note to the client's opportunity in WayHub.
  • Note must include: date and time of session, key discussion points (brief confirmed, communication expectations set, search strategy agreed), any specific client requests or clarifications, client's overall sentiment and readiness, and specific next actions for the team.
  • Update the opportunity's status to "Client Engaged" or "Property Search Commenced" (or the relevant stage).

Trello

  • Go to the client's Trello board and check off "Onboarding Completed" in the checklist.
  • Upload or link the finalised Buyer's Brief to the relevant Trello card and to the client's Google Drive folder.
  • Update the client info card with any final adjustments from the session.
  • In the "Communication Schedule with the Client" card, add a note about the onboarding session and the agreed-upon communication frequency.
Complete WayHub and Trello documentation the same day as the session - not the next morning. Details are forgotten quickly and a late note is less accurate.

Quick Reference Checklist

Use this before and after every onboarding session.

  • Review Client Agreement, Fact Finder, and pre-qualification notes before the session
  • Draft Buyer's Brief ready for review
  • Client Google Drive folder and Trello board open
  • Zoom link confirmed (if virtual)
  • Warm welcome and agenda confirmed with client
  • Buyer's Brief reviewed and finalised with client
  • Communication channel, frequency, and response times agreed
  • Search strategy and process explained to client
  • Client questions answered
  • Next steps confirmed (our actions and client actions, with dates)
  • WayHub note added and lead status updated
  • Trello "Onboarding Completed" checked off
  • Finalised Buyer's Brief uploaded to Google Drive and Trello
