Goal Statement

The purpose of this SOP is to guide David through building and updating a single comparison chart on Yahoo Finance that tracks the top global property stocks across Australia, UK, and the US. This chart is reviewed monthly to monitor the direction of real estate stocks worldwide, helping identify the top of the land cycle and inform when to adjust property strategy for clients.

Importance

Global REIT and property stock performance is a leading indicator of where the broader land cycle is heading. By combining AU, UK, and US stocks onto one chart, patterns emerge that are invisible when looking at individual markets. Completing this review monthly - and sharing the link at catch-ups - keeps the whole team aligned on macro property market direction and supports better timing advice for clients buying, selling, or holding.

Step-by-Step Process

Complete all steps in one sitting. The full process takes 10-15 minutes. Do this on the first business day of each month.

1

Open Yahoo Finance

  • Navigate to finance.yahoo.com in your browser.
  • In the search bar at the top, type the first ticker code: CHC
  • Select Charter Hall Group from the dropdown results (ASX listing).
If multiple results appear, look for the one labelled ASX for Australian stocks, LSE for UK stocks, and NYSE or NASDAQ for US stocks.
2

Set Up the Chart View

  • On the stock page, click the chart area to open it in full screen.
  • Set the following three options on the chart toolbar:
  • Time period: 1 Year
  • Chart type: Area
  • Interval: 1 Day

This gives you a clean 12-month view of the stock's performance that is easy to compare across all stocks.

3

Add All Comparison Stocks

  • Click the Comparison button on the chart.
  • Type the next ticker code from the stock list (e.g. NSR) and select the correct result.
  • Choose the following settings for each new stock:
    • Line style: Medium fat line
    • Colour: A different colour for every stock - do not repeat colours
  • Click Save after adding each stock.
  • Repeat for every stock in the reference table above.
Keep a note of which colour you assigned to each stock. Use the same colour for the same stock every month so the chart is consistent and easy to read at catch-ups.

Suggested Colour Assignments

  • CHC - Blue
  • NSR - Purple
  • AEWU - Green
  • Additional stocks - Red, Orange, Teal (in order)
4

Review the Chart

Before sharing, spend 2-3 minutes reviewing the chart and noting observations. Look for:

  • Which stocks are trending up, flat, or down
  • Any stocks hitting new highs or approaching 12-month lows
  • Whether AU, UK, and US stocks are moving together or diverging
  • Any signs of a broad peak - multiple stocks reversing after highs simultaneously
  • Signs of recovery - stocks bouncing from lows and trending upward consistently
A rapid rise from a 12-month low across multiple markets (e.g. CHC moving from $8 to $12) signals an entry window. Stocks hitting all-time highs and reversing together signals a potential market top.
5

Share the Chart

  • Once all stocks are added and the chart is complete, click the Share button on the chart toolbar.
  • Click Copy link.
  • Paste the link into the monthly catch-up notes or shared Google Drive folder.
  • Share the link with the team at the monthly market review.
The share link opens the live chart for anyone who clicks it - they do not need a Yahoo Finance account. Always use the share button rather than copying the URL from the browser address bar, as the address bar URL may not preserve all chart settings.
