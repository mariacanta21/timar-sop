Suburb Content Memos (Content Gain)

Person Responsible: David / Ben Version: 1.0 Date: May 2026

Why This Exists

Google now has a ranking signal called Content Gain - it can detect recycled AI content and deprioritises it. To rank suburb profile articles (especially in AI tools like ChatGPT and Gemini), each blog must contain genuinely new information that doesn't exist on other websites. Your on-the-ground knowledge as a buyers agent is the competitive edge. This SOP captures it efficiently.

Step-by-Step Process

1

Pick the Next Suburb

Work through the priority list in order. Tick off each suburb once the memo is sent. Aim for 2-3 memos per week - even one per week adds up quickly.

2

Open Whisperflow and Record 60 Seconds

Open the Whisperflow app on your phone or computer. Hit record and speak naturally for around 60 seconds. No script needed - just talk about the suburb the way you would to a client.

The goal is new information that isn't on other websites. Cover as many of these as you can in your 60 seconds:

  • What's changing in this suburb right now (new development, road works, rezoning)
  • What types of buyers are currently active there and why
  • What's happening with rents - up, down, tight vacancy
  • Any streets or pockets within the suburb worth noting
  • Infrastructure coming or recently completed
  • Your honest take - is it a good buy right now and for who
Example: "Sandy Bay is really active right now for owner occupiers coming from Sydney and Melbourne. The streets closest to the water are holding value well. Vacancy is extremely tight - I'm seeing rentals go in 48 hours. There's a new cafe precinct opening on the main strip in the next few months which is bringing more foot traffic. For investors I think Sandy Bay still makes sense for long-term holders but the yield is low - it's a capital growth play. Avoid the main road properties, the traffic noise is a complaint I hear regularly from buyers."
Don't overthink it. Raw, honest, current intel is exactly what Google is looking for. A 60-second rough take from you is more valuable than a perfectly written 1,000-word AI article.
3

Copy the Transcript

Whisperflow automatically transcribes what you said. Copy the full transcript text - you don't need to edit or clean it up.

4

Send to Two Places

Dean (for Cloudflare suburb pages):

Paste the transcript into WhatsApp or email. Label it clearly - e.g. "Sandy Bay memo." Dean will build the suburb profile page from it.

Claude (for blog article):

Paste the transcript into a Claude conversation and say: "Write the Sandy Bay suburb blog using this memo." Claude will produce a finished HTML blog file ready to upload. See the Blog Creation Workflow SOP for the full process.

5

Tick It Off and Move to the Next Suburb

Mark the suburb as done. Move to the next one on the list. Once all 10 are complete, start the cycle again - the intel will be different by then and the refresh is just as valuable.

What Happens After

Once your memo is sent:

  • Dean builds a low-competition suburb profile page via Cloudflare - targeting searches like "Is Sandy Bay a good suburb?" on Google and AI tools like ChatGPT
  • Claude writes a full blog article from your transcript and provides the HTML file ready to add to the website
  • Both pieces are published - they cross-reference each other and build Timar's authority as the go-to source for Tasmanian suburb knowledge

Why Low-Competition Suburb Keywords Work

Searches like "Is Sandy Bay safe?" or "Kingston suburb profile Tasmania" have very low competition - most buyers agents aren't writing about them. That means your articles rank quickly and appear in AI-generated answers on ChatGPT and Gemini. This is how leads come in saying "I found you on ChatGPT."
