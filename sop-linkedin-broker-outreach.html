Goal Statement

The purpose of this SOP is to guide the VA through the weekly process of sending LinkedIn connection requests to mortgage brokers and financial advisors in Tasmania, following up with those who accept, and booking meetings for David with prospects who respond positively. The goal is to build a pipeline of referral partners who can direct suitable clients to Timar.

Importance

Mortgage brokers and financial advisors work with clients who are actively buying property. A strong referral relationship with even a handful of these partners can produce a consistent stream of qualified leads. This outreach process builds those relationships systematically - five connection requests per week creates a compounding network over time without requiring David's direct involvement in the initial contact stage.

Outreach Flow Overview

All outreach follows this sequence. Each step is covered in detail below.

Research - Find leads in Sales Navigator, build the weekly list
Send requests - 5 personalised connection requests per week with note
Track - Update lead tracker to "Contacted"
Follow up - If accepted but no reply, send follow-up script
Book meeting - If they respond positively, get contact info and schedule coffee/tea with David
Nurture - Regular check-ins and engage with their LinkedIn content

Step-by-Step Process

1

Find Leads Using Sales Navigator

Before sending connection requests, you need a list of prospects. Use Sales Navigator to find mortgage brokers and financial advisors in Tasmania.

  • Log in to LinkedIn Sales Navigator using David's account.
  • Go to Lead Filters and apply the following:
    • Title: Mortgage Broker OR Financial Advisor
    • Location: Tasmania, Australia
  • Review the results and select prospects who are not already connected to David.
  • Save their LinkedIn profile URLs to the lead tracker spreadsheet.
  • Aim to have at least 5 new prospects ready each week before starting outreach.
If the existing list already has uncontacted names, skip this step and work through those first before generating new leads.
2

Get the Correct Outreach Script

  • Open the Timar Buyers Agency Google Drive.
  • Navigate to: Sales Referral Process > Scripts
  • There are two scripts - select the right one for this prospect:
    • Script A: Mortgage Brokers
    • Script B: Financial Advisors
  • Keep the script open and ready to copy from.
Do not mix up the scripts. Mortgage broker script references "lender broker partners." Financial advisor script references "financial advisor" partnerships. Double-check before pasting.
3

Send the Connection Request (5 per week)

  • Log in to David's LinkedIn account.
  • Open the first prospect's LinkedIn profile from the lead tracker.
  • Check they are not already connected. If connected, skip to the next name.
  • Click Connect, then click Add a note.
  • Copy the correct script and paste it into the note field.
  • Replace [First Name] with the prospect's actual first name.
  • Review the message quickly - make sure the name is correct and no placeholder text remains.
  • Click Send.

Example Script - Mortgage Broker

Script A - Mortgage Broker
Hi [First Name], I run a buyers agency here in Tassie and I'm hoping to connect with local lenders to explore potential partnerships. We often help speed up the purchase process for our clients - thought it'd be worth connecting and exploring.

Example Script - Financial Advisor

Script B - Financial Advisor
Hi [First Name], I run a buyers agency here in Tassie and I'm hoping to connect with local financial advisors to explore potential partnerships. We often help speed up the purchase process for our clients - thought it'd be worth connecting and exploring.
LinkedIn limits connection request notes to 300 characters. If the script is too long, it will not send. Always check the character count before hitting Send.
4

Update the Lead Tracker

  • After sending each request, immediately update the lead tracker spreadsheet.
  • Mark the prospect's status as Contacted
  • Record the date the request was sent.
  • Repeat Steps 3 and 4 until you have sent 5 connection requests for the week.
Update the tracker immediately after each send - do not batch update at the end. This prevents accidental duplicate outreach.
5

Monitor and Follow Up on Accepted Requests

Check David's LinkedIn messages regularly (at least twice a week) for responses and accepted connections.

Scenario A: Accepted but No Reply

  • If a prospect accepted the connection but has not responded to the initial note, send the follow-up script.
  • Find the follow-up script in the same Google Drive folder: Sales Referral Process > Scripts > Follow-Up
  • Send it as a direct message (not a new connection request).
  • Update the tracker status to Accepted - Followed Up

Scenario B: Responded and Open to Meeting

  • If the prospect has replied and shown interest, respond promptly.
  • Ask for their email address or phone number so David can follow up directly.
  • Pass the contact details to David and confirm a time for a coffee or tea meeting.
  • Update the tracker status to Meeting Booked
6

Prepare David for the Meeting

  • Before the meeting, remind David of the prospect's name, firm, and any notes from the LinkedIn conversation.
  • Ensure David has a printed or digital copy of the Perfect Client Profile to share with the prospect.
  • The Perfect Client Profile explains the type of buyer Timar works with, so the referral partner knows exactly which clients to send through.
The meeting agenda is: introduce Timar, share the Perfect Client Profile, ask about the prospect's client base, explore a referral arrangement, and agree on a follow-up cadence.
7

Post-Meeting Follow-Up and Ongoing Nurture

  • After the meeting, send a brief thank-you message via LinkedIn or email.
  • Schedule a regular check-in with the partner - aim for once a month.
  • Engage with their LinkedIn posts (like, comment) at least once a week to stay visible.
  • If they refer a client, acknowledge it promptly and keep them updated on how that client progresses.
  • Update the tracker with all meeting notes and next follow-up dates.

Weekly Checklist

Complete this checklist each week to ensure the outreach process stays on track.

  • Lead list reviewed - at least 5 uncontacted prospects ready
  • Correct script selected for each prospect (broker vs. financial advisor)
  • 5 connection requests sent from David's LinkedIn with personalised note
  • All 5 prospects marked as "Contacted" in the tracker
  • LinkedIn messages checked for accepted connections and replies
  • Follow-up script sent to anyone who accepted but has not replied
  • Any positive responses escalated to David with contact details
  • Engaged with at least 2-3 existing connections' LinkedIn content
